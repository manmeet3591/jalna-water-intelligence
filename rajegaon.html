The OHT has two discrete probes at approximately 50,000 L (lower) and 100,000 L (upper). Valid states are: Below lower, Between probes, At / above upper, Inconsistent, or Unknown / stale. A continuous percentage such as "73%" is not supported by this sensor configuration and will not be displayed. Probe wiring logic and electronic truth table require field-team sign-off before any level state is shown.
