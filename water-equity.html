A Gini coefficient of 0 means every household receives identical water supply; 1.0 means one household receives all water. Jalna's drought-elevated score of 0.58 reflects severe divergence: wealthier households with private storage or borewell access maintain supply while landless agricultural workers and SC/ST communities face near-zero formal supply.
