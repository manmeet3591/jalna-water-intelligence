What Appears Here and When

This page shows reservoir identity only. Storage figures, fill percentage, surface-area estimates, and seasonal charts will be added only after each metric passes the following gate:

  • The data source is identified and its provenance documented in Data & Methods.
  • The evidence type (OBSERVED / OFFICIAL / ESTIMATED / MODELLED) is assigned and reviewed.
  • At least one independent reviewer has signed off the calculation or calibration.
  • The data honesty label and its definition are confirmed visible on the metric tile.

Demonstration values (7.10 ML capacity, 4.82 ML storage, 67.9% fill) will not appear. These arithmetic figures were generated during prototype design to test the dashboard layout. They are not sensor readings and have no scientific basis as a description of Mandala Talab's current state.

A satellite imagery layer will appear only after scene selection is complete, cloud cover is assessed, water-area extraction is validated, and the resulting boundary is confirmed by a remote-sensing specialist. A generic satellite tile or placeholder outline is not an acceptable substitute.
